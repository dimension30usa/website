About Anurag

From physical rehabilitation to whole-life wellbeing.

Before Dimension 30, Anurag Datta worked in sports physiotherapy, including with the Indian Wrestling Association and Uttar Pradesh's Ranji cricket team.

Working closely with athletes changed the way she understood health. An injury may appear in the body, but recovery does not happen in isolation. Stress, emotional resilience, relationships, sleep, pressure, decision-making and the way a person manages the rest of life can shape how setbacks are experienced and overcome.

Her question became larger than “Where does it hurt?” It became: “What is happening in this person's life?”

That observation grew into a broader practice combining life coaching, meditation and traditional systems of insight—helping people understand their patterns, manage pressure and move through difficult periods with their feet still beneath them.

The Dimension 30 Philosophy

Not every difficult season is meant to disappear.

Astrology at Dimension 30 is not used to promise escape from every uncomfortable period, nor to prescribe endless rituals that supposedly erase what life is asking you to experience.

Some chapters simply have to be lived. Insight helps you understand the terrain: when to act, when to wait, what patterns require attention, where your choices still matter, and how to keep a difficult period from defining what comes after it.

The goal isn't to avoid the storm.
It's to still have your feet beneath you when it passes.
